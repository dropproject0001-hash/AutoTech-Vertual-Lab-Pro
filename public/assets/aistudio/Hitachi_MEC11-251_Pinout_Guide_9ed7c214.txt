HITACHI MEC11-251 ECU BENCH TESTING & PINOUT DIAGRAM
====================================================================
Target Vehicle Line: Nissan Sentra/Sunny/Almera (N16 Series)
Communication Protocol: K-Line (ISO-9141)
Diagnostic Tool: Ancel V7 HD (or equivalent OBD2 Scanner)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
CONNECTOR ORIENTATION & IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
When looking directly inside the pin side of the empty ECU plug chamber 
with the guide slots / locking tabs facing up:

- The connector is split into three main horizontal rows.
- Numbering follows sequential order per block from top-left.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
CRITICAL BENCH PIN CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Connect your jumper lines exactly as mapped below:

1. ECU Pin 67  -->  Constant +12V Power (Battery Positive)
2. ECU Pin 36  -->  Ignition Switch Input (+12V via an Inline Toggle)
3. ECU Pin 48  -->  Ground (Battery Negative & OBD2 Pins 4 and 5)
4. ECU Pin 88  -->  K-Line Diagnosis Data (OBD2 Pin 7)

*Note: Alternatively, Pin 57 can be used as your main chassis Ground instead of Pin 48.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
OBD2 FEMALE HARNESS / DIAGNOSTIC BREAKOUT BOX ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Route the respective jumper wires coming from the ECU into your OBD2 
socket pins where your Ancel V7 HD scanner plugs in:

OBD2 Pin #    | Wire Target (Source)
--------------|-----------------------------------------------------
Pin 4 & 5     | Connect to ECU Ground [Pin 48 / 57] & Battery (-)
Pin 7         | Connect directly to ECU K-Line Data [Pin 88]
Pin 16        | Connect to +12V Fused Battery Positive (+)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
⚠️ BENCH SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- FUSE PROTECTION: Install an inline 5-Amp or 10-Amp fuse on the positive
  lead wire extending directly from your car battery before splitting 
  it off to the ECU's power input pins (Pins 67 & 36).
- INSULATION: Use high-quality female micro-pin sleeves to connect 
  to the gold ECU male pins. Ensure adjacent jumpers cannot make contact 
  with each other, which will short out the internal electronics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
BENCH WAKEUPS AND TEST PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ensure all manual connections match the configuration map above.
2. Hook up your fused leads safely to the 12V car battery terminals.
3. Switch on the power to Pin 36 (Ignition switch state) to trigger 
   the internal processor's main relay logic.
4. Insert your Ancel V7 HD diagnostic interface into the wired OBD2 socket.
5. Manually configure your scanner app to select: 
   Nissan -> Manual Diagnosis -> Sentra/Almera/Sunny (N16 Engine Controls).
